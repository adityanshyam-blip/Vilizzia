FAILURE ARCHIVE

What didn't work, and why it's here anyway.

Fixed position-size bot
RETIRED
Attempted: a trading bot with a fixed trade size regardless of volatility. Why it failed: it blew through drawdown limits in choppy markets. Lesson: led directly to the risk-sizing logic in Survival AI v2.
One-page template for every client
RETIRED
Attempted: reusing one template across early client sites to move faster. Why it failed: clients felt the sites looked generic and interchangeable. Lesson: every build now starts from the client's actual brand.

What this studio believes.

01

Every client project should look like it was designed for that business, never a swapped-logo template.

02

03

A website should do the job it was built for: bring in real bookings, real enquiries, real trust.
